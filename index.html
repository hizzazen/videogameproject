"Find a job you enjoy doing, and you will never have to work a day in your life." - Mark Twain

What happens if you don't even enjoy life?

Reality is broken.

It's depressing, unproductive, disconnected.
Pointless, isolating, unsustainable.

It offers little feedback, little reward,
and little reason to try.

(McGonigal, 2011)

Games have always offered an escape from this

A life of YOUR choosing,
where YOUR actions visibly matter,
where YOU are part of something greater.

...

But what if games could be
more than an escape?

What if your everyday actions fed directly into another world giving you immediate feedback, rewarding your effort, making you the hero of your own adventure?

"they fill our real lives with positive emotions,
positive activity, positive experiences
and positive strength."

(McGonigal, 2011)

To be better than yesterday.
